FAQ

Questions, answered

Is KidShield legal to use?

Yes. Parental monitoring of minor children's devices is legal in most countries. KidShield is designed for parents to monitor their own children's devices with their knowledge.

Will my child know they're being monitored?

That's up to you. We recommend open communication with your children about digital safety. The app can run visibly or discreetly based on your preference.

Which devices are supported?

KidShield works on iOS 12+ and Android 6.0+. The parent dashboard works on any device with a web browser.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes — cancel in one click from your account, no questions asked. All plans include a 30-day money-back guarantee.

Is my family's data secure?

Absolutely. All data is encrypted end-to-end and we never share or sell your information.
